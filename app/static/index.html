Dietitian view

This screen is for the dietitian, not the person following a plan. It lists everyone on the system, how closely each is keeping to their plan, and the reasoning behind every plan that was issued or refused.

Anyone can make the account below, because the system has no authentication: the role is asserted, not proven. A deployment would issue this account to a verified professional. Demonstrating the separation of the two roles is as far as this goes, and the dissertation says so.
